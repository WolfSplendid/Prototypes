Co-op survival · 1–6 players

Keep the lights on. Don't get snuffed.

Team up as a crew of candle-topped toys, relight an abandoned workshop floor by floor, and see how deep you can go before the dark catches up.

How to playMeet the cast

Meet the Wicks

Six toys, six jobs. Mix and match to survive the deeper floors.

Pip

Runner

Fastest feet in the workshop. Burns bright, burns short.

Bolt

Tinkerer

Repairs lanterns in half the time and hums while doing it.

Dribble

Healer

Shares wax with teammates who are running low.

Spots the Snuffer through walls for a few seconds.

Mallow

Slow and squishy, but shrugs off one hit every floor.

Crate

How to play

  1. 1

    Light the lanterns

    Every floor has a handful of dead lanterns. Find them, fix them, and keep them burning.

  2. 2

    Dodge the Snuffer

    Something in the dark wants the lights out. Listen for the draft and stay out of sight.

  3. 3

    Ride the lift

    Once the floor is lit, pile into the lift, pick an upgrade, and head one level deeper.

FAQ

How many players is it?+

One to six. Floors scale with the size of your group.

Is it scary?+

It's spooky rather than scary: cartoon suspense, no gore.

Do I keep my upgrades?+

Upgrades reset each run. Wax you earn unlocks new Wicks permanently.
